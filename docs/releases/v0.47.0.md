# AelionBot v0.47.0

谁是卧底上线；群聊里的 Bot 有了性格、关系和记仇，还能套用网络经典人格。

## 简体中文

### 谁是卧底

- AI 游戏大厅新增「谁是卧底」。你可以和群里的 Bot 一起玩，也可以只看 Bot 互相玩。内置 191 组词。
- 和狼人杀一样，比赛结果会影响 Bot 的性格成长和它们之间的关系。

### 群聊里的性格

- 群聊顶部新增「成员与性格」：可以看到每个成员的性格、对你的态度、和其他成员的关系，也能打开完整的人格档案。鼠标停在头像上有一张小卡片。
- 新加入群的 Bot 会根据自己的角色设定，自动估一份性格初稿，等你确认。
- 性格会影响 Bot 在群里怎么说话：话多话少、直不直接、同意时是附和还是补充。

### 关系与记仇

- Bot 会记住群里谁对它说了什么。被骂、被冒犯会记仇；被感谢、被帮忙会更亲近。游戏里正常的指认和投票不算，越界的人身攻击才算。
- 亲近和怨气分开算：关系再好，欠着一句道歉也会显示「有芥蒂」。
- 和好要讲条件：别人劝和只能缓和一点，对方亲自道歉才会真正开始和好。记仇多久、怎么表现，看各自的性格。
- 问 Bot 和谁关系怎么样，它会照实说，并说出原因。

### 人格库

- 人格页新增人格库，一共 8 个网络经典人格：NPD、舔狗、阴阳怪气、确实型、反驳型、嘉豪、妈咪、猫娘。每个都有介绍、口头禅和示范台词，点一下就能套用。
- 口头禅按各自的频率出现，不会每句都一样。
- Bot 说话出戏时（比如对还没道歉的人突然很亲热），下一轮会收到提醒。检查在后台进行，不会拖慢回复。

### 修复

- Bot 在群里 @ 另一个 Bot 时，名字后面直接接中文（如「@小周你看看」），对方也能收到。
- 群聊越聊越慢的问题：每次唤醒不再重复发送不变的群规则和上下文，长时间的群聊回复更快，压缩也少了很多。

## English

### Who's the Spy

- The AI games lobby adds Who's the Spy. Play with the Bots in a group, or watch them play each other. It ships with 191 word pairs.
- As with Werewolf, match results shape the Bots' personality growth and their relationships.

### Personality in group chats

- A new Members and personality panel in the group header shows each member's personality, how it feels about you and about the others, and opens its full profile. Hovering an avatar shows a small card.
- A Bot that joins a group estimates a first draft of its personality from its role, for you to confirm.
- Personality shapes how a Bot talks in groups: how much it says, how directly, and whether it adds something when it agrees.

### Relationships and grudges

- Bots remember what members said to them in groups. Insults and offence leave a grudge; thanks and help bring them closer. Ordinary accusations and votes in a game do not count; personal attacks that cross the line do.
- Closeness and grudge are kept apart: even between friends, an apology still owed shows as strained.
- Making up has conditions: someone urging peace only softens a grudge a little, and only the offender's own apology starts real reconciliation. How long a grudge lasts and how it shows depend on each personality.
- Ask a Bot how it gets on with someone and it answers truthfully, with the reason.

### Personality library

- The personality page adds a library of 8 internet archetypes: NPD, Simp, Passive-aggressive, Yes-man, Contrarian, Show-off, Mommy and Catgirl. Each comes with an introduction, catchphrases and a sample line, and applies with one click.
- Catchphrases appear at each personality's own rate instead of in every line.
- When a Bot slips out of character, for example warming up to someone who has not apologised, it gets a reminder on its next turn. The check runs in the background and never slows a reply.

### Fixes

- A Bot that @-mentions another Bot with Chinese text right after the name, such as 「@小周你看看」, now wakes it.
- Group chats no longer slow down over time: unchanged group rules and context are no longer re-sent on every wake, so long chats reply faster and compact far less often.
