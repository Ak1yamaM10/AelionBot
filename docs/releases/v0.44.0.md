# AelionBot v0.44.0

这一版加入连接器：在飞书、企业微信或微信里私聊你的 Bot，它在这台电脑上干活，再把结果发回去。群聊里 Bot 也更能分清回复是对谁说的，并按性格说话。

## 简体中文

### 连接器

- 插件页新增“连接器”。每个连接器把一个 IM 账号连接到一个 Bot，所有平台都是从这台电脑主动连出去，不需要公网地址。
- 飞书：可以扫码创建（自动创建飞书应用，扫码的人成为主人），也可以填写自建应用的 App ID 和 App Secret，添加对话框里写了每一步。暂不支持 Lark 国际版。
- 企业微信：填写智能机器人（API 模式、长连接）的 BotID 和 Secret。同一个机器人在别处连接后，这里显示“已在其他设备连接”，不会抢回来。
- 微信（实验）：通过微信 ClawBot 插件扫码登录。只能回复对方发来的消息，可能被微信限制。
- 每个外部私聊是 Bot 的一个工作会话，侧栏里用消息图标标出，消息上注明来自哪个平台。会话的本机权限跟随连接器，在连接器详情里修改。
- 只有主人和主人确认过的人可以使用：其他人发消息会收到 6 位配对码，在连接器详情里允许或拒绝。群聊消息一律忽略。
- IM 里只会收到最终回复和附件。处理期间，飞书在你的消息上显示“敲键盘”表情，微信显示“正在输入”。被新消息打断或手动停止的任务不发消息，失败时发一条说明。
- 需要本机权限时，审批卡片发给主人，可以直接“允许一次”或“拒绝”；没有按钮的平台回复“同意 编号”或“拒绝 编号”。Bot 的提问也会发到 IM，直接回复即可。
- 在电脑上发的消息只在电脑上回复，不会同步到 IM。会话里的定时任务结果会发到 IM。
- IM 里可以用 `/new`、`/stop`、`/status`、`/help`。

### 托盘

- 托盘里一直有 AelionBot 图标，单击打开窗口，菜单里能看到各连接器的状态和“退出”。
- “关闭窗口后保持连接”默认打开：有启用的连接器时，关闭窗口只是隐藏到托盘，连接器继续收发消息。

### 群聊

- 回复某个 Bot 的消息，和 @ 它一样算作对它说话，其他成员只补充它没说到的内容，不再抢着回答。
- 有性格档案的 Bot 在群聊里按性格决定回复的长短和语气，不同意时会直说。没有档案的 Bot 不受影响。
- 模型把发言写成发送参数的 JSON 时，只发布其中的消息内容。
- 群成员满 8 个时，选择列表会说明需要先取消一个。

### AI 游戏

- 结束的对局，战报卡片留在对局结束的位置，之后的消息排在它下面。

## English

### Connectors

- Plugins have a new kind, connectors. Each one links an IM account to a Bot. Every platform connects out from this computer, so no public address is needed.
- Feishu: scan to create (a Feishu app is created for you and whoever scans becomes the owner), or enter the App ID and App Secret of your own app; the add dialog lists every step. Lark International is not supported yet.
- WeCom: enter the BotID and Secret of an AI bot in API mode with a persistent connection. If the same bot connects elsewhere, the connector shows "Connected on another device" and does not take the connection back.
- WeChat (experimental): sign in by scanning through the WeChat ClawBot plugin. It can only reply to messages sent to it, and WeChat may restrict it.
- Each private chat becomes a work session of the Bot, marked with a message icon in the sidebar, and its messages show which platform they came from. The session's local permissions follow the connector and are changed in the connector's details.
- Only the owner and people the owner approves can use it: anyone else who writes gets a six-digit pairing code to allow or refuse in the connector's details. Group chats are ignored.
- The chat receives only the final reply and its files. While the Bot works, Feishu shows a typing reaction on your message and WeChat shows "typing". A task interrupted by a new message or stopped by you sends nothing; a failed one sends a short note.
- When local permission is needed, an approval card goes to the owner with "Allow once" and "Deny"; on platforms without buttons, reply "同意 <code>" or "拒绝 <code>". Questions from the Bot arrive in the chat too and are answered by replying.
- Messages you send from the app are answered in the app only. Results of scheduled tasks in the session go to the chat.
- `/new`, `/stop`, `/status` and `/help` work in the chat.

### Tray

- AelionBot always has a tray icon: click it to open the window, and its menu shows each connector's status and Quit.
- "Stay connected after closing the window" is on by default: with a connector enabled, closing the window hides it to the tray and connectors keep working.

### Group chats

- Replying to a Bot's message addresses it the way an @ does; other members only add what it did not cover instead of answering for it.
- Bots with a personality profile set the length and tone of their group replies by it, and say plainly when they disagree. Bots without a profile are unchanged.
- When a model writes its message as the send tool's JSON arguments, only the message is published.
- When a group already has 8 Bots, the picker says one must be unselected first.

### AI games

- A finished match's report card stays where the match ended, with later messages below it.
