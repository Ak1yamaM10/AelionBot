# AelionBot v0.47.3

## 简体中文

### 群聊节奏与设置

- 每个群可单独设置 Bot 连续发言数量、两位 Bot 的来回轮数、进度消息是否计入上限，以及等待被点名成员的时间。选择不限制时，单轮仍保留 200 条消息的保护上限。
- 群聊设置按基本信息、聊天节奏和智能判断分类，支持管理成员与共享目录；取消群成员数量上限。
- 阅读历史时不再自动跳到底部，改为提示新增消息数量。

### 人格与游戏

- 成员面板可进入人格选择，完整人格档案新增独立的人格库分类；改善根据角色设定估计出生人格时的输出空间。
- 游戏桌面显示 Bot 的真实头像，支持开局打乱座位、点击选择自己的座位，以及仅为本场游戏选择人格预设。Bot 的预设语气与口头禅也会带入游戏。
- 优化夜间行动、目标选择和真人发言的显示；每场结束的游戏都会保留战报卡片，并按实际板子提供角色说明。

## English

### Group conversation pace and settings

- Configure each group's consecutive Bot messages, exchanges between two Bots, whether progress counts towards the limit, and the wait for an addressed member. Unlimited settings retain a safety cap of 200 messages per round.
- Group settings are organised into basics, conversation pace and smart judgement, with member and shared-folder management. Groups no longer limit the number of members.
- Reading earlier messages no longer scrolls to the bottom automatically; new messages are counted instead.

### Personalities and games

- Open personality selection from the members panel and browse the dedicated library in a personality profile. Role-based birth estimates have more room to answer.
- Game tables show each Bot's real avatar. Shuffle seats before a match, click to choose your own seat, and select personality presets for that match only. Bots retain their preset voice and catchphrase habits in games.
- Improved night actions, target selection and human speech presentation. Every finished match keeps a report card, and role instructions follow the selected board.
