# AelionBot v0.47.1

Bot 说话更像人：不再是一串短句和缩写。

## 简体中文

### 说话更自然

- 群聊里去掉了每条发言的字数上限。之前模型为了凑字数会省主语、用缩写，读起来像电报。现在话多话少仍然看性格，但用的是「通常一两句」「聊得起劲时会多说几句」这样的说法，不再是字数。
- 群聊里闲聊要用完整、口语化的句子，不用短语碎片、缩写和列表；只有汇报工作结果时才先说结论、用列表。
- 私聊同样改为用自然完整的句子回答，不再一味求简短。
- 确实型这类本来就话极少的人格不受影响；游戏里的发言仍保留字数限制。

## English

### More natural speech

- Group chat no longer caps each message at a character count. Models met the cap by dropping subjects and abbreviating, so replies read like telegrams. How much a Bot says still follows its personality, now described in everyday terms such as "usually a sentence or two" rather than a number.
- Casual group talk uses natural, complete spoken sentences, without fragments, abbreviations or bullet lists; conclusion-first answers and lists are kept for reporting work results.
- Private chats also answer in natural complete sentences instead of aiming for brevity.
- Personalities that are meant to say very little, such as the Yes-man, are unchanged, and game speech keeps its length limit.
