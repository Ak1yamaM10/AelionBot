# AelionBot v0.46.0

预览里的评论：钉在哪里就在哪里说，Bot 按编号逐条回复，改没改一眼看得到。

## 简体中文

### 评论

- 预览和画布上的“标注”改名为“评论”，标签上显示评论数。工具只剩两个：选取（C）和画笔（P）。
- 选取：网页上点一个元素就钉在它上面，文件上点一下钉在那里，拖动圈出一块区域。画笔随手画，画成直线会变成箭头。
- 每个钉旁边弹出气泡，直接写这里要怎么改，Enter 完成。网页元素的气泡里可以打开样式面板直接调。
- 按数字键 1–9 打开对应的评论，Esc 先关气泡。

### 发送之后

- 钉不会再被清空，发送后变成灰色的“已发送”，Bot 工作时显示“正在改”。
- 每条评论附一张特写图，Bot 能看清每个钉指的是哪里。
- Bot 会按编号逐条回复，没改的也说明原因。回复里的 #1、#2 变成可以点的编号，点一下就在预览里打开那个钉。
- 钉的颜色跟着回复变：绿色是已改，橙色是没改。气泡里能看到 Bot 对这一条说了什么。
- 已发送的钉再写几句，会随下一条消息重新发出。
- 网页预览会留一张发送评论时的画面，Bot 回复后可以点“对比上一版”，左右并排看改动。

### Bot 钉评论

- Bot 可以用新工具 preview_mark 在预览里钉自己的问题，比如“这两种配色选哪个”，你直接在钉上回答，回答会按编号发给它。

## English

### Comments

- Annotations in previews and on the canvas are now comments, with a count on the tab. Two tools remain: Select (C) and Pen (P).
- Select pins a comment on the element you click in a web page, or on the spot you click in a file; drag to mark an area. Pen draws freely, and a straight line becomes an arrow.
- Each pin opens a bubble beside it to say what should change; Enter finishes. For a web page element, the bubble can open the style panel to adjust it directly.
- Number keys 1–9 open that comment, and Esc closes the bubble first.

### After sending

- Pins are no longer cleared. They turn grey as sent, and show in progress while the Bot works.
- Each comment comes with a close-up image, so the Bot can see exactly what each pin points at.
- The Bot answers each comment by its number, and says why when it leaves one unchanged. #1, #2 in its reply become chips that open that pin in the preview.
- Pins take the colour of the reply: green when changed, amber when unchanged. The bubble shows what the Bot said about that comment.
- Adding to a sent pin sends it again with the next message.
- Web previews keep an image from when the comments were sent. Once the Bot has answered, Compare with previous version shows the two side by side.

### Bot comments

- A new tool, preview_mark, lets a Bot pin its own questions on a preview, such as which of two colour schemes to use. You answer on the pin, and your answer reaches the Bot by number.
